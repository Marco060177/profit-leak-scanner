# T11 — Account and channel lifecycle

`Account` is durable. Its new `status` defaults to `ACTIVE`. `PENDING_DELETION` is a controlled, internal-only tombstone; `deletionRequestedAt` records when it was requested. There is no public Account deletion route and no implicit physical purge. The tombstone closes all ChannelConnections, removes Shopify sessions, cancels pending Account notification deliveries, and blocks authenticated Shopify resolution and normal channel jobs. It refuses the transition while an Account delivery is already processing. Account-owned usage, preferences and history remain protected by restrictive foreign keys until a separately approved retention/purge process can dispose of them. Do not mistake tombstoning for completed physical erasure.

Verified Shopify `APP_UNINSTALLED` removes sessions and moves only the mapped Shopify channel to `DISCONNECTED`; repeats are idempotent. The Account, mapping, AccountAiUsage, Account NotificationPreferences and other channels survive. Authenticated reinstall/reauthorization reuses the verified mapping and reactives only `DISCONNECTED` or `REAUTH_REQUIRED`; `PENDING_DELETION` and inconsistent mappings fail closed. Routine ACTIVE lookup remains read-only and transaction-free. A late uninstall webhook for an already tombstoned Account is acknowledged without reactivation.

`SHOP_REDACT` remains Shopify-channel cleanup: shop-owned operational rows, Shopify deliveries and sessions are removed; Account-owned preferences have their Shopify provenance scrubbed. The verified source is disconnected. Repeated redaction is idempotent. Account and unrelated channels survive. The legacy mapping remains for authenticated deterministic reinstall, not as runtime Account authority. A future legal/privacy decision may require mapping erasure after redaction; that would need a separate identity-recovery policy and is not silently invented here.

Notification cron and send paths require an ACTIVE Account and Shopify channel. The Profit Impact measurement scheduler checks the non-creating tenant lookup before Shopify Admin access; disconnected, redacted and tombstoned channels are skipped. Webhook-origin alerts use the same active mapping boundary. Historical delivery attribution is not active authority.

## Deployment and verification

Deploy the additive Account columns via the existing Prisma migration workflow, then deploy the T11 runtime. No data backfill or manual Account status changes are required: existing Accounts default to `ACTIVE`. Do not invoke `requestAccountDeletion` on production without an explicit, separately authorized Account deletion request and a retention/purge procedure. Verify uninstall/reinstall for a dev shop, session removal, preservation of Account preferences/usage, and cron skip for disconnected channels. No migration or production-data mutation was performed during implementation.
